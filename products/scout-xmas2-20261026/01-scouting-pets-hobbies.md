# Scouting — pets-and-hobbies lane — 2026-10-26

## Handoff
- **Verdict: no product in this lane cleanly passes L-009 + L-002 + L-008.** I checked ~30 candidates across dog, cat, coffee, cooks/bakers, gamers, golf, camping, readers. Almost every item is a $5–15 commodity, or a $20–40 item that is either bulky (>500 g), electronic/branded, or has an identical listing far cheaper. Fewer than 5 pass is fine per the brief; here 0 pass and 2 are "near misses".
- **Near miss 1: sourdough bread-baking kit** (home bakers). Best demand in the lane: RankHero "sourdough starter kit" Dec 90,500 vs Jul 18,100 (5x), Nov 60,500 (measured, third-party, US 100%); Amazon kit ASINs ~10k units/mo at $19.99–29.99 (asinsight, estimated); TikTok Shop "Starter-To-Loaf Kit" 25.8K sold at $56.89. Fails today: identical 2-basket + tools kits sit at $19.99 (Amazon) / $26.75 (Walmart), and the Made-in-China quote for a basket + tools set is $10.80–11.42 (MOQ 1 set), which lands at ~$22.50 = 58% of $39 on the China route.
- **Near miss 2: padded zip book sleeve** (book readers). Gift-obvious (appeal 5) and light (~150 g, ~$2 unit at MOQ 300), but Amazon demand is tiny ("book sleeve with zipper" 351 searches/wk) and TikTok sellers sit at $16–22 handmade with max 930 sold. Plain sleeve fails L-009; it only works with an owned print, which is a custom run (out of scope).
- **Pets are a commodity trap in this lane.** TikTok Shop pet: top dog/cat toys $5–15; Amazon cat toys and dog toys top ASINs $4–14 at 10–30k units/mo (asinsight Aug 2026). Pet outfits need sizes; pet "gift" items like paw-print kits are $15–17. Dog advent calendars: demand is real but a Nov-only spike (RankHero Nov 110,000, Dec 49,500, Jan 1,600) with tiny TikTok sold counts (23, 95, 182) and a Dec 1 deadline: dropped.
- **No CJ SKU or US-warehouse stock could be verified**: CJ search pages redirect to a captcha (confirmed again 2026-10-26 on /search/banneton.html), and CJ blog lists name no SKUs. Every cost below is a Made-in-China / retail listing price (estimated), not a CJ quote. The owner needs a free CJ account to check US stock before either near miss is revisited.
- Reusable data: TikTok Shop `shop.tiktok.com/us/k/<keyword>` worked for dog-lovers, cat-gifts, white-elephant-gifts, golf-accessories, camping-gifts, book-sleeve, sourdough-starter-kit, wireless-meat-thermometer, dog-advent-calendar. Many slug variants 404 (suction-cup-dog-tug-toy, dog-pulling-toy, duck-butt-dog-toy). asinsight: many "gifts-for-X" pages 404; the ones that work are listed in Sources.
- **Leads for other lanes (not verified, outside my lane):** TikTok Shop "white-elephant-gifts" page shows Screaming Goat Plush $29.95 (15.9K sold, 4.7), Gejoy Fake Poop $13.25 (18.2K), Middle Finger in a Box $10.99 (11.6K), Spider Prank Box $17.99 (4.2K), HornBlasters fart spray $25.99 (12.1K, branded). ## Brief
- Lane: pets-and-hobbies, from `products/scout-xmas2-20261026/STATUS.md`. Christmas gifts that are funny or useful for broad passion groups (dog/cat owners, coffee drinkers, home cooks/bakers, gamers, golfers, campers/hikers, book readers).
- Constraints: US market; CJdropshipping (L-006); must arrive before Dec 25 with store launch ~Nov 10–15 (L-008); $19–49 or bundle; landed <= 1/3 of price including 35% duty (assumed) + $1 clearance on China parcels (L-002); Stripe 4.5% + $0.30 (assumed). No coin cells, kids' toys, food/treats, licensed brands, fragile glass or size variants.
- Rejected ideas in `memory/MEMORY.md` were respected (lamps, projectors, satin sets, book nooks, necklaces, lick/snuffle mats, pet hair removers, paw cleaners, frothers, wine openers, etc.).
