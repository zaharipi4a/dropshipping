Why this name

  • It names the moment the kit is for: the minute a nurse clocks off and has to sleep.
  • "Club" makes it an identity night-shift people want to be part of, which helps organic content.
  • It can stretch to other night-shift healthcare workers later. Launch stays nurses-first.
  • Of three names with a free-looking .com, it has the lowest trademark-confusion risk.

Name checks

  • offclockclub.com looks available (registry lookup returned "not found"; not a guarantee).
  • No brand or trademark hits in web search.
  • Instagram and TikTok handles couldn't be checked: both sites blocked the agent. Check by hand.
  • A USPTO trademark search is required before anything is printed.

Alternates: Sunrise Sleeper (crowded "Sunrise" sleep marks), Day Sleep Club (a SLEEP CLUB bedding mark exists). Avoid "Wind Down Kit": Pinch Provisions already sells one.

Who buys

Two buyers, one product

The Day Sleeper

Buys for herself · January onward, Nurses Week

  • US RN, LPN, CNA or tech, 23–40, works 3×12s from 7 p.m. to 7 a.m.
  • Drives home into full sun and tries to sleep 9 a.m.–3 p.m. in a bright, noisy house.
  • Already owns a flat mask that leaks at the nose or presses on her eyes.
  • Objections: "Ice rollers are a $7 Amazon thing." "$59 is a lot." "Another nurse gimmick?"
  • Online right after shift (~9 a.m. ET) and before shift (~5–6 p.m.).

The Gift Giver

Buys for a nurse · Nov 1 – Dec 3, graduations, Nurses Week

  • Partner, parent or friend of a night-shift nurse.
  • Wants "a gift they'll actually use", not another mug, pen or badge reel.
  • Worries: will it arrive in time, and is $59 too much?
  • Searches "gifts for nurses", which jumps about 6× in December.
