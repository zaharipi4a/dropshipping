# 01b - Search demand check (US) - 2026-10-26

## Handoff
- **Coverage is thin.** Free tools gave real numbers for only some keywords. Ahrefs, Semrush, Ubersuggest, keywordtool.io, MerchantWords and Glimpse were blocked or empty (see last section). Every figure below is a third-party estimate, never Google Ads data. Nothing was invented. `n/f` means not found.
- **Group A (nurse wind-down kit):** demand is large and clearly seasonal.
  - "sleep mask": about 60.5K US Google/mo (RankHero, estimated), steady all year with a December peak.
  - "gifts for nurses": about 6.6K US/mo (RankHero), but swinging 5.4K to 33K. The December peak is about 6x the trough, and there are smaller peaks in April and May for Nurses Week.
  - "nurse gifts": about 5.4K US/mo, peaks Dec 14.8K and May 12.1K.
  - "ice roller": Amazon US about 12.2K searches/week (about 52K/mo by my x4.3 arithmetic). Exploding Topics shows 40-50K with growth, but its geography is unstated.
  - "blackout sleep mask": about 1.0K US Google/mo (RankHero).
  - Night-shift long-tail terms: n/f.
- **Group B (curl kit):** demand is real but mostly head-term, with no evidence for the niche terms.
  - "heatless curls": Amazon US about 14.7K/week (about 63K/mo by my arithmetic) in July 2026. MerchantWords showed 11.8K/mo, but this was only a search-snippet quote I could not verify.
  - RankHero's Google figure is 165K but with no US split, so treat it as global.
  - TikTok #heatlesscurls is 5.4B (2023) to 8.9B (2024) views.
  - "heatless curling rod": about 1.1K Amazon/week.
  - "dance competition hair", "cheer hair curls" and "competition curls": **n/f**. Any niche-segment demand case rests on assumption, not data.
- **Trend direction (12m):** RankHero shows "heatless curls" fading from 165K to 110K between Jul 2025 and Jul 2026, i.e. past peak. This is low confidence (see caveats). "sleep mask" is flat.
- **Caveats:**
  - RankHero is an Etsy-oriented tool. Its headline figures sometimes disagree with its own monthly tables, and its trend tables run past the "updated" dates shown, so they may be modeled. Treat them as order-of-magnitude.
  - Amazon figures are WEEKLY. Monthly = weekly x 4.3 is my arithmetic, not a source figure.
  - Amazon volumes are not comparable to Google volumes.

## Method and conventions
- Tools used: WebSearch and WebFetch only, on 2026-10-26.
- Labels: `estimated` = third-party tool model. `measured` = none obtained, because no Google Ads or Search Console access. `derived` = my arithmetic on a sourced number.
- Seasonality is read from RankHero monthly tables (Sep 2025 to Aug/Sep 2026) unless noted.

## Sources blocked or empty
- Ahrefs keyword generator (https://ahrefs.com/keyword-generator): page loads but shows no results without login or captcha.
- MerchantWords (www.merchantwords.com/search/us/...): "Verifying your browser" interstitial. Numbers quoted above come only from search-snippet text and are unverified.
- RankHero: "Not enough data yet" for ice roller, ice roller for face and heatless curler. 404 for sleep-mask-for-sleeping and earplugs-for-sleeping.
- Glimpse (meetglimpse.com/trend/heatless-hair-curler/): HTTP 410 Gone.
- ASINSIGHT: 404 for nurse-gifts. Returns only one weekly snapshot per keyword, with no chart values in the text.
- Not attempted or not accessible: Semrush, Ubersuggest, keywordtool.io, Google Keyword Planner and Google Trends (login or JS required), TikTok hashtag pages (JS and login). Dance, cheer and night-shift keywords were not found in any public source reached.
