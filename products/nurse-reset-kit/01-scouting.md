# Scouting — 2026-10-26

## Brief
- **Seed:** none. I searched all categories for products that are selling now (Oct 2026) and looked at which categories earn the most.
- **Market:** US. The owner is outside the US, so the plan does not depend on TikTok Shop. TikTok Shop sold counts are used only as proof of demand.
- **Budget:** under $500 total. Marketing is organic first (TikTok, Reels, Pinterest, Shorts). Landed cost should be ≤ $15, with 1 or very few SKUs. The product must be filmable at home and show its result in under 3 seconds of video.
- **Price:** sweet spot $25–60. Landed cost must be ≤ 1/3 of price (L-002). Each product needs ≥ 3 proof signals (L-004) and a segment gap (L-005).
- **Excluded:** supplements, medical devices or claims, trademarked goods, weapons, battery-heavy or regulated electronics, fragile or oversized items.

### Landed-cost assumptions used throughout (important, new since 2025)
- **US de minimis is gone for China-origin parcels** (effective 2025-08-29). Since 2026-03-01, duty is a % of declared value with no flat-fee option ([Reuters via TradingView](https://de.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com%2C2025%3Anewsml_L1N3TR13G%3A0-us-ends-tariff-exemption-for-all-low-value-packages), [CMGM](https://www.cmgm.net/us-de-minimis-exemption-ended-2026-china/)). The exact current rate is not confirmed, so I **assume duty = 35% of product cost** on China-shipped orders.
- **China→US small-parcel shipping (CJPacket-class), ≤200 g:** about $4.80–5.50, 7–15 days (`estimated`). Basis: CJ's older $4.14/100 g figure plus CJ's own "+~25% since tariffs" note ([CJ blog](https://blog.cjdropshipping.cn/detail/1304239081684701185), [CJ tariff post](https://blog.cjdropshipping.cn/detail/How-CJdropshipping-Helps-You-Adapt-to-New-U-S--Tariffs), [OrderTracker](https://www.ordertracker.com/couriers/cj-packet)).
- **CJ US warehouse:** 2–7 day delivery, $0.50 outbound fee. Stocking your own goods needs at least 10 pcs per variant and 100 pcs total ([AliDrop CJ review](https://www.alidrop.co/blogs/cj-dropshipping-review), [Spocket on CJ](https://www.spocket.co/blogs/cjdropshipping-overview)). **Consequence:** a ≤10-day promise means either (a) choosing SKUs CJ already holds in its US warehouse (check after login), or (b) a later 100-pc stock-in of about $300–450, which does not fit the first $500. **Until then, honest shipping copy is "7–15 business days"** (L-003).
- **Fees:** 3% of price (per brief).
