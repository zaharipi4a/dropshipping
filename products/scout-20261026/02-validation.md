## Shared assumptions (apply to every combo)

### Duty: re-checked
The scout assumed 35% because the details were unconfirmed. Here is the current position, per [Zonos US tariff tracker, updated 2026-10-14](https://zonos.com/us-tariff-updates) and the [Stran & Co 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001872525/000121390026087791/ea0301159-10q_stran.htm):

| Layer | Status for China-origin parcels, Oct 2026 |
|---|---|
| IEEPA tariffs (incl. China "fentanyl"/reciprocal) | **Ended** 2026-02-24 (`measured`, Zonos) |
| Section 122 10% global surcharge | Ran 2026-02-24 → **expired 2026-07-24** (`measured`) |
| New Section 301 "forced-labor" tariff | **12.5% for China** since 2026-07-24 (`measured`, Zonos; the [Helen of Troy 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000916789/000091678926000138/hele-20260831.htm) also lists China at 12.5%) |
| MFN base duty | Applies to postal parcels since 2026-07-24. Examples: curlers HTS 9615.90.20 ≈ **8.1%** ([wove](https://tariffs.wove.com/us/tariff/9615.90.20)), 9615.90.40 ≈ 5% (`measured`, third-party aggregator) |
| Legacy 2018–19 Section 301 China lists (7.5–25%) | Not confirmed for these HTS codes. **`assumed` still in force, at 0–25% depending on HTS** |
| De minimis | Still suspended for all countries (EO of 2026-02-20). Postal parcels must be **duty-prepaid (DDP)** and entered by the buyer or a licensed broker. Mandatory Entry Type 13 starts **2027-01-20** (`measured`, Zonos) |

**Result:** the total duty stack is about **20–46% of declared value**: MFN 5–8% + 12.5% + 0–25% legacy. **I keep 35% as a conservative midpoint (`assumed`).** I also add **$1.00/parcel for DDP and clearance handling** (`assumed`; forwarders charge it now that postal entries need a broker). The scout's numbers left this out. Downside case: if legacy Section 301 is 25% on the item, duty is about 46%, which adds only about $0.45 per kit at these product costs. Duty is a small line. **Shipping is the line that decides margin.**

### 2. Stage-Ready Curl Kit → teen/adult dancers & cheer — MAYBE

**Economics**

|---|---|---|

| Duty 35% | $1.09 | $2.03 |

| Shipping (~150 g / ~260 g) | $4.80 | $6.40 |
| Insert | $0.30 | $0.30 |
| **Landed** | **$10.29 (35.5%)** | **$15.53 (31.7% ✓)** |
| Payment 4.5% + $0.30 | $1.61 | $2.51 |
| Apps | $0.30 | $0.30 |
| Returns 5% | $1.45 | $2.45 |
| **Contribution margin** | **$15.35** (52.9%) | **$28.21** (57.6%) |
| **Break-even CPA / ROAS** | **$15.35 / 1.89** | **$28.21 / 1.74** |

**Realistic CPA (`assumed`):** $15–25 on cold beauty-accessory traffic. Break-even is at the bottom of that range, so the single kit is marginal on paid ads.

**Demand proof:** strong for the generic product. Top Amazon ASINs sell about 10k/mo; #heatlesscurls had 224M views in 30 days; Kitsch did about 11.4k TikTok Shop orders in 4 weeks (see 01-scouting.md). **Segment demand is not proven.** My searches for dance- or cheer-specific heatless use returned only generic tutorials ([Rapunzel kit](https://www.rapunzelofsweden.com/product/heatless-curls-kit), [Teleties guide](https://teleties.com/blogs/teletimes/3-easy-steps-to-create-heatless-curls-overnight)). The only segment signal is the scout's single ChalkBucket thread.

**Product-fit risk (new, important):** competition and cheer curls are usually tight, uniform and heavily sprayed (hot rollers or sponge rollers). A single satin rod gives **soft, loose waves**, as reviewers and the kit makers themselves describe ([Rapunzel](https://www.rapunzelofsweden.com/product/heatless-curls-kit): "soft curls, waves"). Calling it "stage-ready" could over-promise, which leads to returns and bad comments. **Fix before any GO:** test a sample on long hair through a few hours of movement. If it doesn't hold, reposition to "rehearsal, recital, Nutcracker and photo-day curls", or add satin flexi-rods for tighter ringlets. Flexi-rods add about $0.80 and 40 g.

**Competition:** Kitsch sells a satin set at about $16 with 29.5k TikTok units, plus Rapunzel, Invisibobble and Mermade, and 100+ generics at $5–18. The dance-bun-kit sellers (So Danca, Tech Dance) don't sell heatless products. The angle is open, but $29 against a $16 branded set needs a strong segment story and the 2-pack.

**Supplier risk:** cheap and abundant. Visible AliExpress listings have low review counts. **US-warehouse SKU: not found.** CJ search sits behind captcha, and no CJ US listing showed up in the index. The only US-shipped units are third-party eBay sellers (e.g. Indiana, about 1 week; [eBay](https://www.ebay.ca/itm/116706120102)). Honest delivery is **7–15 business days** until the owner checks CJ's "US warehouse" filter or does a later 100-pc stock-in (~$300–450, outside the current budget).

**Compliance and IP:**
- **CPSIA:** whether something is a "children's product" depends on stated intent, packaging, marketing, and how consumers commonly see it ([CPSC](https://cpsc.gov/cpc)). A large share of competition dancers and cheerleaders are ≤12, and dance moms buy for them (`assumed`). Marketing to "dance moms" therefore risks being classed as a children's product, which means third-party lab testing, a CPC and tracking labels (`assumed` $300–800 testing, unaffordable). **Positioning has to be teen 13+ / high school, college and adult dancers, studio teachers.** That shrinks the segment. No minors in any content.
- **Kitsch:** no heatless-curler trademark or patent litigation found. Their only recorded patent suit is about a hair-tie tool (US11206908B2, settled 2023; [Patsnap](https://www.patsnap.com/resources/blog/litigation/kitsch-llc-v-lauren-peterson-lolly-llc-hair-tie-patent-patsnap/)). The generic satin rod is sold by 100+ listings, so design-patent risk looks low (`estimated`). Rules: never use "Kitsch" in copy, tags or ad keywords; don't copy their print patterns, packaging layout or the product name "Satin Heatless Curling Set". The brand stage should run a USPTO design-patent search for "heatless curling".

**Creative potential:** 5/5. The unwind reveal (rod off → curls drop) is a proven 1–3-second payoff, and the owner can film it on themselves if they have long hair.

**Timing:** Nutcracker shows in early to mid December and holiday gifting, then competitions Jan–Jun. Realistic first sales come in **January**.

## What would make us kill it later (kill criteria for the test phase)
**Pre-launch**
- CJ landed quote (incl. duty and DDP) is **> $13.50** at $34 → reprice to $36 (re-check conversion) or kill. Above $15 → kill.
- A sample roller rusts or leaks, the mask leaks light, or the kit looks cheap next to a $34 price → change supplier once, then kill.
- The test parcel to a US address takes **> 15 business days** → don't launch with the holiday angle. Fix shipping first.
- Store isn't live by **Nov 20** → drop the Christmas-gift angle and relaunch around self-purchase plus Nurses Week (May).

**Organic test (no spend)**
- After 20 posts / 3 weeks across TikTok + Reels: no post above 5k views **and** link CTR under 0.5% → change the angle once (e.g. gift-giver → self-care POV).
- After 40 posts / 6 weeks: fewer than 3 organic sales → **kill**, and test Curl Kit in January instead.

**Paid (only after Gate C, $10–15/day)**
- $50 spent (about 3× target CPA) with 0 purchases on an ad set → kill that ad set.
- Blended CPA **> $18** (break-even) after $150 → stop paid ads; return to organic.
- Blended ROAS **< 1.9** over 2 weeks with no upward trend → stop.

**Operations**
- Refund or complaint rate **> 8%**, or **> 10%** of orders delivered beyond the stated window → pause sales.
- Any platform rejection for health claims → rewrite to cosmetic-only. Two rejections → pause the channel.
