# Supplier options for a German B2C store (run from Bulgaria)

Date: 2026-10-27. Author: sourcing researcher. Market: DE/DACH, EUR, custom-coded site (Stripe/PayPal/Klarna), budget under EUR 500 in total.
Labels: `measured` = read on the cited page today, `estimated` = third-party or vendor claim, `assumed` = my inference, `unknown` = not findable without a login. Nothing here is legal or tax advice.

## Handoff

- **Integrate FIRST: CJdropshipping.** No fixed fee found in any source read (CJ's own EU blog says you pay product plus shipping per order; `estimated`, no pricing page found). It has a public API (products, per-warehouse stock, orders, webhooks, `measured` on the docs landing page) and costs EUR 0 to test, which matters on a EUR 500 budget. Weak point: EU/DE stock is per SKU, and quoted 3-7 day delivery is `estimated` (vendor blog). Reliability signals are mixed (see CJ section).
- **Integrate SECOND, driven by the product picked:** BigBuy if the product sits in its catalogue (gifts/home/pets, Spain/France stock, neutral packaging, API in the Ecommerce pack). It costs about EUR 89 setup + EUR 89/month (`estimated`, third-party; official pricing page returned 403), so pay only once a product is chosen, and cancel after a test month if it disappoints. If we instead sell our own designs (L-009 lever), the second adapter is **Printify** (public API for custom stores confirmed, EU print providers in DE/PL/LV/CZ, free plan) or **Gelato** (public API, sandbox, webhooks).
- **Skip for now:** Zendrop (Germany average 9.5 business days, ships from China, extra EU duty charge, so it fails L-008); Eprolo (no EU stock or delivery data found); Spocket and Syncee (built for Shopify/WooCommerce apps; no API mentioned on pricing pages; Syncee order webhook for custom servers exists but tracking must be entered by hand); AliExpress (no verified API or EU-stock route for a custom site); Kaufland/Otto (sales channels, not supply).
- **vidaXL (dropshippingXL):** the best-documented API of all (Basic auth, order POST, tracking by polling, sandbox, `measured`) and a low fee (EUR 30/month, 2023 review only, `estimated`). But the catalogue is furniture/garden/pet houses (bulky, low margin) and vidaXL sells the same items to consumers itself; branded vidaXL packaging is a documented complaint. Treat it as a candidate for outdoor/pet products only, third in line.
- **Owner to open now (free, 15 minutes each):** (1) a CJ account, then use "Get API Key", filter the catalogue by Germany/EU warehouse, and list 10 candidate SKUs with stock, price and weight; (2) request sandbox access for vidaXL (email dropshipment@vidaxl.com, per their API page) only if a pet/outdoor candidate appears; (3) a Printify account (free plan) to read provider production times for one blueprint. Do NOT pay BigBuy, Zendrop or any plan before a product passes Gate A.
- **Checks on the CJ trial:** number of SKUs with Germany/EU warehouse stock in our categories; the "Query Inventory by Product ID" sub-warehouse field; one paid test order to a German address and the measured business days; whether the parcel carries a CJ invoice or packing slip; whether CJ issues a VAT invoice to a Bulgarian company, and the return address for EU stock; whether CJ handles GPSR labelling for EU-warehouse SKUs.
- **Cross-cutting, to verify with the Bulgarian accountant before ANY paid account:** supplier VAT treatment on invoices to a Bulgarian entity (supplier from Spain/NL/DE charging local VAT vs 0% intra-EU with a valid Bulgarian VAT ID), whether a Bulgarian VAT number must exist first (vidaXL's terms require a valid tax number), OSS registration, and who is the GPSR responsible person and the VerpackG-obligated party for supplier packaging.
- **Build advice:** write the order integration behind a small supplier-adapter interface (`createOrder`, `getStock`, `getTracking`) so adding BigBuy or a POD later is days, not weeks. vidaXL needs polling for tracking, CJ and Gelato and Printful offer webhooks.
- **Evidence gaps:** BigBuy's official pricing, FAQ and API docs were blocked (Cloudflare 403 or 400 for agents); CJ's catalogue, EU stock and fees are behind login; Gelato's dashboard docs were not fetchable. Every price below marked `estimated` must be confirmed in the supplier's own account.

## Supplier sections

### 1. CJdropshipping (recommended first)
- **Catalogue and stock.** CJ claims 400,000+ products and EU warehouses in the UK, Germany, Netherlands and Poland; the page names Berlin among storage locations (https://www.cjdropshipping.com/blogs/dropshipping-knowledge/European-Dropshipping-Supplier, estimated, vendor blog). Another CJ blog lists one Germany warehouse among two US and three China sites (found via search, estimated). Third-party sources quote 2-3 days (Berlin/Rotterdam), 3-7 days (EU) and 6-20 days overall; they conflict (estimated). No SKU-level stock counts for our categories were readable (catalogue behind captcha/login).
- **Cost.** The CJ blog says you only pay product and shipping per order (estimated). No pricing page found. Duty/handling on China parcels into the EU: to verify (LEARNINGS reference block).
- **API.** https://developers.cjdropshipping.com/en/api/introduction.html (measured): product interface and synchronisation guide, authentication (access-token, API key), per-warehouse inventory ("Query Inventory by Product ID" sub-warehouse list added 2025-11-19, "Get Storage Info" added 2025-11-18), shopping/orders guide, webhook guide with order status, logistics/tracking, stock and product updates (https://developers.cjdropshipping.com/en/api/webhook/). Create-order parameters and rate limits were not readable in the pages fetched.
- **Branding.** CJ custom packaging and insert cards exist (see knowledge/suppliers.md CJ packaging row). Whether the parcel shows a CJ invoice: unknown, test with the first order.
- **Returns, VAT, GPSR.** Not found; all behind login or unclear. A hustlegotreal guide only says to confirm who pays for returns and what paperwork is in the parcel.
- **Reliability.** Trustpilot (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/cjdropshipping.com, read 2026-10-27): rating unavailable "due to a breach of our guidelines", fake reviews removed, 13,472 reviews, 666 in the last 12 months; sampled complaints: items listed in stock but not in the warehouse, orders unshipped 10-12 days, scripted support, wrong items. Shopify app listing 4.9 from about 2,500 ratings (self-selected, estimated).
- **Why first.** Zero fixed cost, documented API, covers both China-direct and EU-warehouse SKUs, already L-006 tier 1. The risk is that few relevant SKUs have EU stock, which the trial will quantify.

### 2. BigBuy (second, conditional on product)
- **Catalogue.** 168,000+ items (https://dodropshipping.com/bigbuy-pricing-plans/, article from 2023, modified 2026-08-05) versus 400,000+ SKUs (https://www.copyfy.io/en/blog/bigbuy-review, updated 2026-10-03). The two figures differ, so confirm in the account.
- **Stock and delivery.** Warehouses in Spain and France; Germany indicative 3-5 days, Q4 +1-2 days, delays rarely communicated (Copyfy, estimated). Not demonstrated by us, so L-008 is met only after a test parcel.
- **Cost.** Ecommerce pack EUR 89/month (EUR 74.17 on annual billing), Marketplaces EUR 119, EUR 89 setup on every pack, unlimited dropshipping orders on the first two; the B2B pack allows only three purchases per month (dodropshipping.com; the EUR 89 monthly figure is confirmed by https://help.woosa.com/en/articles/70027-how-does-my-bigbuy-dropshipping-account-work). Orders are paid from PayPal or a Moneybox with a minimum EUR 300 deposit (Woosa, measured). Plain arithmetic: setup + first month = EUR 178 (assumed from those figures), plus the EUR 300 deposit if not paying by PayPal, which is most of the EUR 500 budget. Use PayPal per order and do not pre-fund the Moneybox until a product is validated.
- **API.** BigBuy's own page https://bigbuy.eu/en/api_bigbuy.html (403 to me; seen only in search results) lists catalogue sync, orders, shipping-cost and tracking modules and says it is for developers only. Interactive docs at http://api.bigbuy.eu/public/doc/ returned 400/redirect to agents. The pack descriptions say API, FTP CSV/XML are in the Ecommerce pack (estimated).
- **Branding.** Neutral packaging included; logo packaging is extra; invoice under your name and no BigBuy mention on the parcel (Copyfy, estimated).
- **Returns.** BigBuy blog (found via search): distributors set their own returns policy and must offer withdrawal; BigBuy does not repurchase returned goods, offers a free pick-up for defective or incident orders, 2-year warranty, RMA tool. Refund delays of 15-30 days reported (Copyfy). Who pays ordinary return shipping: not confirmed.
- **VAT.** Not confirmed from any source. To verify with the accountant: invoice with Spanish VAT vs 0% to a valid Bulgarian VAT ID.
- **Reliability.** Trustpilot (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/bigbuy.eu, 2026-10-27): 2.9, 4,743 reviews, 222 in last 12 months; complaints: surprise EUR 89 charges, refunds, stock visible only after payment, delays (the star breakdown shown is inconsistent with the 2.9 score). A Spocket (competitor) blog on 2026-05-14 reported 3.7. Direction is down, treat with caution.
- **GPSR.** No BigBuy-specific statement found. BigBuy blog says products carry a 2-year warranty; labelling/responsible person: to verify per product.

### 3. vidaXL dropshippingXL
- **API (best documented).** https://habatradingbv.b2bwave.com/pages/8-api (measured): production https://b2b.vidaxl.com, sandbox https://sandbox.b2b.vidaxl.com by emailing dropshipment@vidaxl.com; Basic auth with account email and API token; max 1 request per second; products up to 500 per GET; `POST /api_customer/orders` with product_code, quantity and address; orders accepted by the API sit as unpaid until you pay them manually in the portal (a manual step that breaks full automation unless wallet payment can be automated: to verify); tracking number appears at status 5 (Sent) and is polled by order reference; carriers include PostNL, DPD, GLS, UPS, FedEx; invoices fetchable by API. The hosting domain is a B2BWave reseller page for Haba Trading BV, the terms entity named by the search result, so confirm on vidaXL's own site.
- **Terms** (https://habatradingbv.b2bwave.com/pages/177-drop-shipping-terms-and-conditions, measured): subscription per delivery country; no minimum order per item; DDP; dispatch within 2 working days of full payment; delivery times are estimates; prices exclude VAT, added at checkout; valid tax number verified automatically; 14-day withdrawal; you bear return costs when you handle returns; refunds cover product price only and go to your wallet; vidaXL's liability capped at the purchase price; vidaXL accepts product-defect responsibility as required by law and decides recalls (a helpful GPSR signal, but not a responsible-person statement).
- **Cost.** EUR 30/month, PayPal only, API and hourly stock feed included (https://ecommerce-platforms.com/articles/dropshippingxl-by-vidaxl-review, last updated 2023-08-23, estimated, stale).
- **Catalogue.** 90,000+ products (same review, 2023). The domain www.dropshippingxl.com returned "The site is currently unavailable" when fetched today, so confirm the programme is open to new Bulgarian sellers.
- **Branding.** "You can't brand your product packaging" (review); Trustpilot reviewers complain about vidaXL-branded packaging that lets customers buy direct.
- **Reliability.** Trustpilot dropshippingXL 2.5 from 46 reviews, newest 2026-03-24 (measured). Consumer-side vidaXL reviews show delivery overruns (e.g. 4-6 days promised, 11 days actual), anecdotal.
- **Fit.** Pet houses, outdoor and garden items are a realistic lane; margin and branding are the issue. Not for gift-sized items.

### 4. Print-on-demand: Printify, Printful, Gelato
- **Printify** (https://developers.printify.com/, read first 100k chars): public REST API, personal access token (valid 1 year) or OAuth (6 h access tokens, app review up to a week), global 600 req/min, webhooks need `webhooks.write`, "Connect your own e-commerce channel" explicitly supported for custom stores; catalogue shipping profiles list individual EU countries. EU print providers in UK, Germany, Czech Republic, Poland, Latvia; production times per provider visible in the catalogue (https://printify.com/print-on-demand/europe). Free plan (pay per order) plus a Premium subscription; GPSR settings exist per store in the dashboard (help.printify.com via search). Orders section of the docs and DE delivery figures not read.
- **Printful** (https://developers.printful.com/docs/): orders created as draft then confirmed, `external_id` mapping, webhooks (order created/updated/failed/canceled), shipping-rate and tax-rate endpoints, 120 req/min. https://www.printful.com/shipping: 2-5 business days to produce and fulfil, Europe standard 5-20 bd, express 1-3 bd, EU shipping from USD 4.99 per T-shirt (measured). Fulfilment in Latvia and Spain (third-party), and EU-fulfilled orders to EU states avoid customs (Printful blog via search). Printful's Europe-wide standard range of 5-20 bd is too wide for L-008 without a test parcel.
- **Gelato**: API at https://order.gelatoapis.com with X-API-KEY, sandbox with auto-cancelled test orders, webhooks with Production Status Update (https://developers.gelato.com/docs/webhooks, via search results). "Most orders produced and delivered within 3-5 days" is a vendor claim (https://www.gelato.com/print-on-demand/europe). Pricing page 403.
- **Why POD.** It is the L-009 lever (own design), EU-produced so no customs, and the packaging/returns questions are the same across the three; confirm plan costs and a blank/branded packing-slip option in each dashboard. VAT and GPSR for POD: to verify; POD vendors typically act as manufacturer on their own blanks, but that is `assumed`.

### 5. Zendrop
- Germany average 9.5 business days (https://support.zendrop.com/en/articles/8487356-zendrop-shipping-times-by-country, updated 2026-05-21, measured); products from China ship worldwide, US-supplier products only inside the US (https://support.zendrop.com/en/articles/12118720-where-zendrop-ships-from-and-international-delivery-times). EU customs duty USD 5.90 per order (USD 3.90 duty + USD 2 handling) from 2026-10-26, not refundable (https://support.zendrop.com/en/articles/15626451-eu-customs-duty-on-orders-shipping-to-the-eu, measured). Plans USD 59/79/199 (third-party). Skip: fails L-008.

### 6. Spocket and Syncee
- Spocket (https://www.spocket.co/pricing, measured): USD 39.99 (25 products) to 299.99; 7-day trial; branded invoice in all plans; no API mentioned. For a custom site we would need a supplier-side workaround, so skip unless a specific EU supplier is only reachable there.- Syncee (https://syncee.com/pricing, https://help.syncee.com/en/articles/12960517-order-synchronization-for-custom-platforms-as-a-supplier): Free to USD 99.99 plans, 0% transaction fee on paid plans; for own-server stores an order webhook on new paid orders exists but must be enabled by support@syncee.com, and tracking sync only supports Shopify, Ecwid, BigCommerce and Wix, so on a custom site tracking is entered by hand. The article I found is titled "as a supplier", so whether it applies to retailers on our side is not confirmed. Skip.

### 7. Eprolo, AliExpress/Cainiao
- Eprolo (https://eprolo.com/de/dropshipping-suppliers-germany): promotional page, mentions an API and branding services, names no EU warehouse, no delivery times, no prices. Skip until EU stock is shown.
- AliExpress: DS Center is a research tool; third-party apps (DSers) use AliExpress APIs; IOSS cannot be declared on AliExpress (Shopify forum thread). No official docs read. Skip.

### 8. German/Polish wholesalers, marketplaces
- Directory entries only (https://www.lieferanten.de/produkt-dropshipping.html, https://hustlegotreal.com/de/dropshipping-lieferanten/de/): Deuba (Saarland; garden, household, toys, sport, pets), Nedis (NL), ZooDrop (pets), Gallay (jewellery, CSV feed), exertis Connect (Saarbrücken; ~10,000 products, dropshipping and private label). None verified: no terms, feeds or delivery data found. Worth one email each if a category (pets, garden) turns out to be the winning lane. Polish B2B wholesalers connect through Shoper/Apilo integrations with individually negotiated terms (https://www.shoper.pl/integracje-hurtownie); no DE shipping terms found.
- Kaufland and Otto partner programmes are sales channels. Not researched as suppliers (no relevance to supply).
- Legal frame for any of them (Händlerbund, https://ohn.haendlerbund.de/recht/rechtsfragen/dropshipping-vertraegen-retouren-produkthaftung, measured): the merchant is liable to the customer for defects even if the dropshipper caused them; customers return goods to the merchant's address (a second return address may be added); product labelling needs manufacturer name, address and electronic contact, plus an EU-based responsible person for non-EU manufacturers; contract points to agree with the supplier: delivery times, quality, returns handling, transport damage, data protection. Put these into every supplier contract check.

## Open questions for the accountant and for supplier accounts
1. Does a Bulgarian company buying from a Spanish/Dutch/German supplier and selling B2C to German consumers need a Bulgarian VAT ID before registering? (vidaXL requires a valid tax number.)
2. Which invoice address and VAT treatment on supplier invoices; do parcels go to a German consumer under OSS?
3. Return address: customers return to the merchant by default (Händlerbund); does the owner use a supplier return address (CJ, BigBuy pick-up) or a German forwarding address? Impressum address is needed regardless.
4. GPSR responsible person for CJ EU-warehouse SKUs and BigBuy products; VerpackG for supplier packaging.
