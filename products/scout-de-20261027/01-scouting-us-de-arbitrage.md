# Scouting — us-to-de-arbitrage lane — 2026-10-27

## Handoff
- **Lane verdict: the US-to-Germany gap is mostly closed.** Almost every US-viral gadget I checked already sits on German marketplaces (Amazon Marketplace, Otto, Kaufland, eBay, Manomano) via Chinese sellers at about the same floor as in the US. Source: billiger.de price comparison, one search per product (links in the tables). Only 1 of 12 products checked clears the gates, and only conditionally.
- **Only conditional pick: 5-blade rotary hand-crank grater with suction base ("Trommelreibe"), EUR 39.90.** US proof is strong (TikTok Shop US: 531.3K, 58.7K, 16.0K and 8.7K sold at $30–50). Germany already sells it at EUR 37–41 (NutriChef at Saturn, Orion on Amazon.de, eBay sellers), so this is price-tolerance proof, not a gap. It passes L-002 only at EUR 39.90 and only if an EU-stock unit costs ≤ EUR 8.9 (assumed numbers, no quote yet). Differentiation 3, the lever is German recipe content (Reibekuchen, Käsespätzle, Raclette), EU stock and free replacement drums.
- **Second pick: none qualifies.** Reserve only: cocktail smoker gift kit (US Dec peak is 5x summer), which needs an EU-stock unit ≤ EUR 10.1 that I could not find. Its differentiation is 2.
- **Re-check of the US near misses for Germany: all still fail.** Knife sharpener (DE floor EUR 17–25, HORL premium EUR 139–400), AirTag/RFID wallet (floor EUR 8–25, German brand slimpuro), Wichteln dice (EUR 7–11), scroll ring (3 DE listings but electronics compliance and CM fail), book sleeve, sourdough kit, desk boxing bag, Pilates board (Merach 4-in-1 at EUR 29.99 on Otto, Aldi sells a EUR 199 reformer).
- **Tooling:** billiger.de/search?searchstring=<german keyword> is readable and indexes Amazon Marketplace, Otto, Kaufland, eBay, Manomano, voelkner, with prices and shop counts. Amazon.de (503), idealo (blocked), Kaufland (403), Otto (400), eBay.de (403), geizhals (403), BigBuy (403) and asinsight/RankHero DE pages (404) are not readable. Mydealz loads but shows no temperatures. So no Amazon.de "bought in past month" data and no Meta Ad Library data exist in this file.
- **German TikTok Shop is not our price band.** Kalodata/FastMoss summaries show DE top sellers are appliances (17.1% of GMV, Ninja/Dreame/Shark) and cheap consumables (April 2026 top-5 average price EUR 13.83).
- **Suppliers (all unit costs `assumed`, catalogs are behind logins):** CJ (free, Frankfurt warehouse exists, per-SKU stock to verify), BigBuy (EUR 89/mo + EUR 89 registration per one third-party source; another says no fee; 3–5 days to DE, API yes), eComZone (Slovenia, CSV/XML/API, 2–5 days, Shopify app delisted, viability unverified). China-direct adds a ~EUR 3 per-item duty from 2026-07-01 (to verify) and 7–15 days.
- **Recommendation to the orchestrator:** do not spend owner money on this lane's products until another lane produces a product with a real German gap. If the grater goes forward, the gate is a quoted EU-stock SKU ≤ EUR 8.9 plus a sample, before any site build.

## Brief
Germany/DACH, EUR prices incl. 19% VAT (margins on net = price ÷ 1.19), German-language store, PayPal/Klarna/cards, EU-stock suppliers preferred (≤ 5 business days), budget under EUR 500, organic-first. Lane: products proven in the US (TikTok Shop US sold counts, Amazon US estimates) that are underserved in Germany. Criteria: break-even ROAS ≤ 2.2 and CM ≥ EUR 12 (L-002), ≥ 3 proof signals (L-004), recipient test (L-009), appeal ≥ 4, differentiation scored, timing (L-008), supplier integration cost (L-006). Margin math uses the placeholder payment fee 2.5% + EUR 0.30 and 8% returns from LEARNINGS (both `assumed`).

## Scout's top pick and why

**Pick 1 (conditional): 5-blade rotary grater at EUR 39.90, positioned on German dishes.**
- It passes L-002 on assumed costs (CM EUR 16.15, BE ROAS 2.08), L-001 (the demo is the proof), L-009 (recipients are obvious), appeal 4 and timing (evergreen with Q4 lift).
- It is honest to call it a price-tolerance play, not a gap: Germany already sells it at EUR 37–41 and generics at EUR 13–31. Differentiation 3 depends on content and a better offer, which cost nothing to test organically.
- Gates before any build: (a) a quoted EU-stock SKU with landed ≤ EUR 14.3, i.e. unit ≤ EUR 8.9 with EUR 4.90 shipping; (b) a sample to check blade quality, since US reviews report broken blades; (c) a Meta Ad Library check in Germany by hand (blocked for agents); (d) design-patent check on the drum-and-suction-base layout (`unverified`).

**Pick 2: none qualifies.** Reserve: cocktail smoker kit, only if an EU-stock kit at unit ≤ EUR 10.1 with a visibly different design is quoted by about Nov 6 (organic test then runs Nov 9–Dec 10). Differentiation is 2 and there are 12 German listings, so it is not a pick.

**What the lane found for the orchestrator:** German marketplaces absorb US trends within months through Chinese Marketplace sellers, so "unserved in Germany" is rare. The levers that remain are German-language content, EU stock and speed, and our own design (POD). Compare with the other three lanes before committing.
