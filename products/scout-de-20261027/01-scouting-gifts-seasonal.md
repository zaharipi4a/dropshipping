3. No BigBuy or EU wholesaler for book nook kits found (searches 2026-10-27).

**EU landed-cost block (EUR 39.90 VAT-inclusive, DE 19%):**
- Net = 39.90 / 1.19 = **EUR 33.53**.
- Landed (assumed) = unit 8.50 + DE parcel 4.50 + packaging 0.50 = **EUR 13.50** (valid only if CJ Frankfurt matches factory price).
- Payment fee on the gross amount = 2.5% x 39.90 + 0.30 = **EUR 1.30** (assumed placeholder).
- Returns 8% of net = **EUR 2.68** (assumed).
- **CM = 33.53 - 13.50 - 1.30 - 2.68 = EUR 16.05; break-even ROAS = 33.53 / 16.05 = 2.09.**
- Break-even landed for ROAS 2.2: CM needed 15.24, so landed <= 33.53 - 1.30 - 2.68 - 15.24 = **EUR 14.3**.
- Sensitivity: unit EUR 12 (EU markup) gives landed 17.00, CM 12.55, ROAS 2.67, **fails**.

**Compliance block (compliance-officer owns it; not legal advice; all items to verify; costs assumed):**

| Item | Why it applies | Effort / cost (assumed) |
|---|---|---|
| ElektroG / WEEE (stiftung ear) | A kit with an LED module is likely electrical equipment; seller placing it on the German market must register with stiftung ear, and a non-German seller needs an authorised representative in Germany (to verify) | Registration fee and rep, about EUR 100-400 per year, plus take-back/system participation (to verify) |
| BattDG | Batteries are "not included" per GIGA, which may avoid the battery duty if none are shipped; if batteries are packed in, registration applies (to verify) | EUR 0 if no batteries; otherwise register and join a take-back system |
| CE: EMC and RoHS (LVD not applicable for low-voltage DC, to verify) | LED/touch circuit | Supplier declaration of conformity and test report, about EUR 0-300 if the factory has one (to verify) |
| GPSR | EU responsible person, manufacturer data, German safety text on the listing | Listing work; EU responsible person service about EUR 50-150 per year if the supplier is outside the EU |
| Toy/age check | Labelled 14+, adult hobby kit, small parts; not a toy under the Toy Safety rules (to verify) | Label 14+ and small-parts warning |
| VerpackG / LUCID | Packaging licence for sales to DE (to verify per supplier) | Small annual fee |

Precedent: MEMORY rejected electric/battery items (mug warmers, heated vests, wine opener) mainly for mains power, lithium cells and Li-ion shipping. A battery-free-shipped LED module is lighter, but ElektroG registration and an authorised representative still apply, so the precedent is only partly lifted. Kill criterion (see Handoff): drop if total registration + CE + EU-rep cost is above about EUR 150, or any step is not doable before launch within the EUR 500 budget.

Honest odds: low. Three separate unknowns (EU-stock SKU at <= EUR 14.3, compliance, copyright) must all resolve favourably.
