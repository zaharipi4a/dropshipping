Suppliers (L-006):

Integration: no subscription (third-party reviews); a "Retailer API" exists in the integrations menu (details not read, to verify); per-order fees not published. Custom packaging: CJ offers it (MEMORY: "as low as one unit", unverified).
2. **Made-in-China bulk factories** (not allowed under tier rules unless the owner stocks from China, so "China-direct only if timing and duty allow"): H&H Industrial USD 6.72-8 (MOQ 100), Shantou Linda USD 10.20-11.40 (MOQ 60-90) per knowledge/suppliers.md; MOQ and EU duty (flat ~EUR 3 per item interim, to verify) make a pre-stock plan EUR 600+, over budget.
3. No BigBuy or EU wholesaler for book nook kits found (searches 2026-10-27).

EU landed-cost block (VAT-inclusive EUR 39.90, DE 19%):
- Net price = 39.90 / 1.19 = **EUR 33.53** (measured arithmetic).
- Unit EUR 8.50 (assumed, only if CJ Frankfurt matches factory price), DE parcel EUR 4.50 (assumed), packaging EUR 0.50 (assumed) = landed **EUR 13.50**.
- Payment fee 2.5% + EUR 0.30 = EUR 1.14 (assumed placeholder per LEARNINGS).
- Returns allowance 8% = EUR 2.68 (assumed).
- **CM = 33.53 - 13.50 - 1.14 - 2.68 = EUR 16.21; break-even ROAS = 33.53 / 16.21 = 2.07** (passes only on these assumed inputs).
- Break-even landed cost for ROAS 2.2 at EUR 39.90 is about EUR 14.5. If CJ Frankfurt unit + ship is above that, the combo fails. At a more realistic unit EUR 12 (EU markup) landed is EUR 17: CM = 12.7, ROAS 2.64, fails.
- Not a pick until a real quote exists.

3. **Printify EU providers**: not researched in this run.

EU landed-cost block (EUR 29.90):
- Net = 29.90 / 1.19 = **EUR 25.13**.
- Poster EUR 5.70 (estimated) + DE shipping EUR 4.00 (assumed) + packaging EUR 0 = landed **EUR 9.70**.
- Payment fee 2.5% + 0.30 = EUR 1.05 (assumed); returns/reprint allowance 4% = EUR 1.01 (assumed; personalised goods are normally exempt from withdrawal, to verify).
- **CM = 25.13 - 9.70 - 1.05 - 1.01 = EUR 13.37; break-even ROAS = 1.88.** Passes L-002 only at EUR 29.90 or more; at EUR 24.90 CM falls to about EUR 9 and fails.
- Weakness: price is above the visible eBay market (EUR 14-40), differentiation 2-3, proof thin, decoration. Not recommended as a lead product.
