## A. CJdropshipping (free account) — 15–20 min
Search each keyword, set the warehouse filter to Germany / Europe, then for each hit write down:
- Product name and link, unit price (EUR or USD), variants
- Warehouse (DE, other EU, or China) and stock quantity
- Weight (g) and packed size
- Shipping to a German postcode (e.g. 10115 Berlin): cost, method, estimated delivery days, handling days
- Any per-order or processing fee, minimum order, and whether branded/custom packaging is possible

Keywords: "cat window perch", "cat window hammock", "window bird feeder", "book nook kit", "disposable toilet brush", "toilet cleaning wand disposable", "rotary cheese grater", "drum grater". Also anything that looks like it ships from a DE/EU warehouse in our categories.

Also test the account for the API: on the developer page, note whether API access is instant and free.

## B. Amazon.de — 15 min
For each of the 4 keywords (German terms: "Katzen Fensterliege", "Vogelfutterhaus Fenster", "Book Nook", "Einweg Toilettenbürste", "Trommelreibe"):
- Top 10 results: price, rating count, rating, "x+ im letzten Monat gekauft", seller (Amazon / marketplace / brand)
- How many of the top 10 are marketplace sellers that look like China importers (generic brand names)?

Discovery (the part our agents cannot do): in Bestseller and "Neuheiten" lists (Haustier, Küche & Haushalt, Garten, Sport, Auto), and in "Movers & Shakers" (Bestseller-Aufsteiger), note 5–10 items that are rising fast, cost EUR 15–60, and are not obviously from a big brand. Send the names and links.

## C. Demand and ads — 10–15 min
