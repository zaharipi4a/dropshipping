## Brief

Home, kitchen and living lane for Germany/DACH: household helpers, kitchen gadgets, organisation, cleaning, bathroom, cosy living, small DIY. German consumer, EUR VAT-inclusive prices (net = price / 1.19), German store, PayPal/Klarna/cards, owner is a fluent German speaker, budget under EUR 500, organic-first on TikTok/Reels/Pinterest/Facebook. Criteria: L-002 (CM >= EUR 12, break-even ROAS <= 2.2), EUR 15-80, >= 3 proof signals with arbitrage allowed (L-004), recipient test and 3-second demo (L-001, L-009), differentiation as a score, timing per L-008, supplier tiers per L-006. Avoid: coin cells, toys needing CE/EN71, food, supplements, health claims, licensed brands, fragile glass, sized apparel, Li-ion-heavy items unless clearly shipped. Rejected ideas in `memory/MEMORY.md` were respected (none re-proposed; the US-rejected "retractable/other" items are not repeated; see "Screened out" for items the new market might have changed).

## Unit-cost ceiling (assumed cost block, applies to every combo below)

Assumptions (all `assumed` until a supplier quote and the owner's Stripe/PayPal rates exist): shipping to DE EUR 4.50 for a light parcel (BigBuy example is EUR 6), packaging/insert EUR 0.50, payment fee 2.5% of gross + EUR 0.30 (L-002 placeholder), returns allowance 8% of net, no customs duty (EU stock), CJ per-order service fee assumed 0 (to verify; not sourced); BigBuy adds about EUR 3 per order if 30 orders/month (copyfy example, to verify). Shipping EUR 4.50 is the optimistic end of the stated EUR 4.5-6 range and no parcel weight has been measured, so every "pass" below is shipping-sensitive.

| Price (incl. 19% VAT) | Net | Max unit cost for CM >= EUR 12 | Max unit cost for break-even ROAS <= 2.2 (binding) |
|---|---|---|---|
| EUR 29.90 | 25.13 | 5.07 | 5.6 (CM 12 binds: 5.07) |
| EUR 34.90 | 29.33 | 8.8 | 7.5 (ROAS binds: 7.5) |
| EUR 39.90 | 33.53 | 12.5 | 9.3 (ROAS binds: 9.3) |
| EUR 49.90 | 41.93 | 20.0 | 13.0 (ROAS binds: 13.0) |

Ceilings use shipping + packaging of EUR 5.00 (a heavier parcel lowers them by the same amount).

Rule of thumb for this lane: unit + shipping + packaging must stay under about 33% of the VAT-inclusive price (EUR 29.90: ceiling 10.07 = 33.7%; a landed 10.00 = 33.4% already sits at the edge). Anything bulky (> 500 g, shipping above EUR 6) or electric (Li-ion, WEEE, CE, BattDG) loses on top of this.
