## Brief

Home, kitchen and living lane for Germany/DACH: household helpers, kitchen gadgets, organisation, cleaning, bathroom, cosy living, small DIY. German consumer, EUR VAT-inclusive prices (net = price / 1.19), German store, PayPal/Klarna/cards, owner is a fluent German speaker, budget under EUR 500, organic-first on TikTok/Reels/Pinterest/Facebook. Criteria: L-002 (CM >= EUR 12, break-even ROAS <= 2.2), EUR 15-80, >= 3 proof signals with arbitrage allowed (L-004), recipient test and 3-second demo (L-001, L-009), differentiation as a score, timing per L-008, supplier tiers per L-006. Avoid: coin cells, toys needing CE/EN71, food, supplements, health claims, licensed brands, fragile glass, sized apparel, Li-ion-heavy items unless clearly shipped. Rejected ideas in `memory/MEMORY.md` were respected (none re-proposed; the US-rejected "retractable/other" items are not repeated; see "Screened out" for items the new market might have changed).

## Scout's top pick and why

**Honest verdict: fewer than 2 qualify. There is 1 conditional candidate and no clean pick.**

- **Conditional candidate: Disposable toilet-wand starter kit (EUR 29.90, family set EUR 34.90).** Reasons: only combo whose assumed numbers clear L-002 (CM EUR 12.07, break-even ROAS 2.08); a 3-second demo (head drops off without touching); a broad self-purchase audience; a repeat-purchase refill pack; arbitrage proof of 210.6K US TikTok sales against thin, brandless German offers. Reasons for caution: zero German sales or search proof; the kit only just passes margin (one euro of unit cost fails it); the cheapest identical German listing is partial (refills only), so differentiation 3 rests on an unseen competitor field; heads may contain a cleaning agent (Detergents/CLP labelling) and any "spülbar/flushable" claim is off-limits in DE; German buyers' plastic-waste sensitivity is a positioning risk (assumed, untested); it is a utility, not a gift (appeal 4).
- **Conditions before it goes to Gate A (validator/owner):** (1) an EU/DE-stock SKU quoted at unit <= EUR 5.0 (kit) with weight and a test parcel showing <= 5 business days to DE; (2) the owner hand-checks Amazon.de for "Einweg Toilettenbürste" (review counts, "gekauft" badges, brands) and Google Trends/Keyword Planner for DE demand, since both were blocked here; (3) head composition (sponge vs preloaded cleaner) and the supplier's GPSR/EU responsible-person details; (4) the owner accepts a disposable-cleaning-product brand. If (2) shows a strong Amazon.de seller above EUR 25 or no search demand, drop it.
- **No second pick.** The electric mill pair (appeal 5, but 10+ German sellers and brands, CM EUR 11.81) and the magnetic spice tins (floor EUR 19, CM EUR 10.07) fail on the numbers, and the own-design tea towel (differentiation 5) fails on margin. I would rather return one conditional than dress up a failing product.
- **Recommendation to the orchestrator:** this lane is structurally weak at EUR 30-35 because EU shipping alone is about 15% of price.
