One Christmas product survives, and only if it ships from the US

Three scouts covered cozy home, personal gifts and gadgets. A validator then stress-tested every candidate on margin, real appeal and one hard question: can it reach a US doorstep by Dec 25? Shipping from China takes too long for a store launching Nov 10–15. Only the aurora lamp, shipped from US stock, gets through.

RankHero, 2025–26, global (54% US) estimated. The peak is still ahead of us, and it falls about 70% in January.

Why it fits what you asked for

  • Makes sense on sight. One photo of a ceiling full of northern lights reads as "cozy Christmas gift". No explaining needed.
  • Works as a gift and for yourself. It's both a present and the "Christmas-time feel" in your own room.
  • A video-first product. The lights-off, lights-on moment is a 3-second hook.
  • Arrives in time. US stock with a measured 4.85-day average delivery on Doba.

The honest weaknesses

  • A commodity. Identical lamps sell for $13–14 on TikTok and eBay. We win only on content, styling and trust.
  • Not CJ. Doba needs your OK as an exception. Its API plan costs $150/mo, so you'd enter orders by hand (~3 min each).
  • No branded gift box. Doba can't add inserts, so the gift note would be digital.
  • Safety checks. The remote must not use a loose coin cell (Reese's Law). Marketing must stay adult-only.

Aurora or sunset lamp from China

No-go 2026

The same demand as the pick, but the real lamp weighs ~450 g, not 250 g. That pushes landed to 38–46%. With China shipping, the last safe order is about Nov 30, which leaves no time for a free organic test first.

Jewelry case, engraved necklace

No-go

The travel case has no dropship route, and its passing price is unverified. Printify's engraved necklace lands at 35%, and the proof we found covers other necklaces, not this one. CJ's necklace comes pre-engraved, not with the buyer's name, which kills the gift point.

What you need to do · by Mon Nov 2

Four checks decide it

None of these costs anything except the $0.99 trial and samples. Bring the answers back and I'll run the brand and growth plan right away.

  1. Free CJ account: search "aurora projector", "northern lights projector" and "ocean wave projector" with the US Warehouse filter. If one costs ≤ $11.16 with US shipping, we use CJ instead and need no exception. Also search "satin pillowcase eye mask set" for Valentine's.
  2. Doba $0.99 trial: for item D01027HNF0W, note the price (must be ≤ $11.66), the stock (want 50+) and the remote's battery type. Check that the box doesn't say "kids", and that manual orders and blind shipping (no supplier branding) are allowed.
  3. Decide: is Doba OK as an exception to "CJ only", with you entering orders by hand for about $30/month?
  4. Samples: order one lookalike lamp to yourself for filming (~$15), and one Doba test order to a US address ($10.17) to measure real delivery time.
