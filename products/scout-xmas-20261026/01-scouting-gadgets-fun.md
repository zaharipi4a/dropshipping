# Scouting (lane: gadgets-fun) — 2026-10-26 (revised after QA round 1)

## Handoff
- **Lane result: one candidate survives, and it is conditional.** Christmas book nook kit (adult 14+, wooden miniature scene with LED), $59 gift-boxed. Everything else in this lane was dropped for proof, appeal or economics (table below).
- **Timing conclusion (L-008): HOLD the book nook for next season unless an in-stock CJ US-warehouse SKU is found.** I found no US-stock SKU (CJ search is behind a captcha; the only US-stock hint is a stale, unverified eBay listing). Realistic launch is about Nov 10–15 (brand, copy, store and organic test still ahead). China routing assumes 3 days processing + 10–20 days transit (assumed, nothing demonstrated), so the latest order date is about Nov 29–Dec 1. That leaves a ~2-week selling window with no organic-test time. If the owner's CJ account shows a US-warehouse book nook SKU (2–7 days, per AliDrop, unverified), the window extends to about Dec 15–18.
- **Book nook pick is conditional on CJ landed ≤ $19.67 (incl. gift extras)** to meet L-002 at $59. My estimate is $22–28 landed (37–47%, estimated), so as it stands it FAILS L-002. Needs a CJ quote on weight and DDP shipping.
- **Demand is right for Christmas (estimated, RankHero):** "book nook kit" peaks in December at roughly 4x the April–July level; "christmas book nook" has 63% of yearly searches in Oct–Dec, with the peak in November.
- **Proof stack is three independent channels:** Amazon unit estimates (asinsight), a Meta advertiser with 724 active ads (BrandSearch, estimated revenue $0.55–1.0M/mo), and sold-out Christmas kits at a retailer (Scottish Book Trust). Ad age (30+ days) is NOT verified; one competitor shows a 64-day top ad.
- **Obvious appeal 4/5:** a lit Christmas scene is understood from one photo, but it is a 4–8 hour build, so it is a "project gift", not an instant gift.
- **Power and age:** listings I read use AAA/AA batteries, not coin cells, batteries not included, age 14+. Never accept a coin-cell version.
- **IP risk:** Rolife and Cutebee artwork is copyrighted and white-label kits copy it. Get an exclusive or generic Christmas scene from the factory in writing.
- **Other lane findings:** light gadgets (frother, fidget, diamond painting, embroidery) have $5–17 Amazon anchors, so they cannot reach $25+ without a filler bundle.

## Brief
Owner wants an obvious Christmas present or a self-purchase for the "Christmas-time feel". The nurse kit was rejected as contrived. US market, custom site + Stripe + CJ (L-006), under $500, organic-first. Hard constraint: arrive before Dec 25. Price $25–60, landed ≤ 1/3 (L-002), duty assumed 35% plus $1 per parcel, payment fee 4.5% + $0.30. Lane: giftable gadgets, kitchen, hobby and family fun for adults. Excluded: coin cells, kids' toys, food, heating, licensed designs.

Method: asinsight.com (Amazon unit estimates), RankHero (seasonality, estimated), BrandSearch (ad counts), retailer pages, Made-in-China. Amazon Best Sellers, Walmart product pages, CJ, AliExpress search and the Meta Ad Library were blocked or login-only. Many asinsight keyword pages return 404.

Labels: `measured` = read from a page; `estimated` = third-party model or my arithmetic; `assumed` = my assumption.
