## Scout's top pick and why
**Satin Sleep Gift Set.** The widest obvious Christmas appeal in the lane (a gift for her, partner, sister or mom), the strongest independent sales proof (Amazon ~10k/mo, a branded set with 2,238 reviews, holiday sets by Slip and Blissy), light and cheap per unit. Weaknesses: China shipping (tight window), a crowded $6–15 market so the offer must be the gift box and card, and avoid hair or skin benefit claims and the word "silk" unless the fabric is mulberry silk. The travel jewelry case is the add-on or second offer. Nothing else in this lane passes.
