Owner wants an obvious Christmas present or self-purchase for the "Christmas-time feel", with no contrived micro-niche. US market, custom site + Stripe + CJ API, under $500, organic-first. Must arrive before Dec 25. Price $25–60, landed at most 1/3 of price (L-002), at least 3 proof signals (L-004). Lane: personal gifts, self-care, accessories. Assumptions: duty about 35% of product cost plus $1 clearance per parcel for China-shipped goods; payment fee 4.5% + $0.30; China ship 7–15 business days plus 2–3 days processing (estimated).

## Scout's top picks and why
1. **Satin Sleep Gift Set** — widest obvious Christmas appeal in the lane (gift for her, partner, sister), strongest independent sales proof (Amazon 10k/mo, brand sets with 2,238 reviews, holiday editions by Slip and Blissy), cheapest per unit and light. Weakness: no US fulfillment, so the cutoff is about Nov 28–30; the competitive set is huge at $6–15, so the offer must be the gift-box experience, not the product. Avoid "silk" unless the fabric is mulberry silk. Avoid hair or skin benefit claims.
2. **Engraved Bar Necklace Gift (Printify, US)** — the only US-fulfilled item I found that works with a custom site API, no duty, order cutoff in mid-December likely (verify). Weakness: borderline L-002 (35% on Premium), Etsy price anchor $20, and proof from Amazon is weak (520 units avg). Treat as the late-window companion offer.

Not recommended: travel jewelry case as a standalone (price anchor $10–16). It could be an upsell inside combo 1.
