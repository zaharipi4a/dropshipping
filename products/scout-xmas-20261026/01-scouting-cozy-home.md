## Scout's top pick and why

1. **Aurora projector lamp as a gift-ready "Night In" box** (combo 1, or the $49 Glow Kit, combo 5). It has the deepest proof in the lane: units on Amazon, 34.6K and 22.6K sold on TikTok Shop, 33.9k weekly searches, a December demand peak and a stable $29–44 price band where unbranded sellers sell. It makes obvious Christmas sense ("the northern lights in your living room" / "cozy glow"), demos in 1 second (switch on, wall colours move), is light, USB-powered and unbreakable. Weak points: a $9–13 commodity floor on Walmart/TikTok, no verified US warehouse, and no ad-longevity check.
2. **3-in-1 sunset/aurora/ocean lamp** (combo 2) at $39. Same box and card; it hedges the aurora with the most viral shape. If the owner prefers one SKU family, run both under one "glow lamp" collection and let the organic test pick the winner.

Open items for the validator (all `unverified`): CJ SKU quote and US-warehouse stock for a plain-USB aurora lamp; remote battery type (reject CR2025/CR2032); FCC ID if Bluetooth; a Meta Ad Library check for "aurora projector" and "sunset lamp" ads running 30+ days; Q4 2025 sales history (FastMoss/Kalodata, login).
