# Scouting (lane: cozy-home) — 2026-10-26 (rev 2, after Stage QA round 1)

## Handoff
- **Top pick (conditional): a USB aurora (northern-lights) projector lamp sold as a gift-ready "warm glow" box at $39 (assumed).** Appeal 4/5. It only works if two things check out: (1) a quote with unit cost <= $4.07 on the China route (break-even for landed <= $13.00, i.e. <= 1/3 of $39), or a US-stock wholesale cost that lands <= $13.00; (2) a route that actually arrives before Dec 25 (below).
- **Timing is the key risk.** China route (CJ per-order fulfilment): STATUS.md assumes ~2 business days (bd) processing + 15 bd transit, so the latest order for a Dec 23 delivery (2-day buffer) is **Nov 30** (arithmetic in the Timing table). No lead time is demonstrated for this SKU on CJ (captcha). The only demonstrated figure, Global Sources 14–21 days, is a factory lead time and pushes the latest order to **Nov 11–18**, at or before the ~Nov 10–15 launch. So the lamp on a China route is a timing risk with 2–3 weeks of selling at best.
- **A US-stock route exists but is NOT CJ.** Doba (US wholesale/dropship platform) lists in-stock ships-from-US aurora/"northern lights" ocean-wave projectors, galaxy projectors with Bluetooth speaker and ~10 sunset lamps; the one product page I could read says handling 2 bd (recent average 1 bd) and delivery 3–5 days via GOFO. Prices and stock are behind sign-up; plans cost $29.99–59.99/mo (TechRadar, Sep 2026, conflicting sources). **L-006 exception, needs owner approval at Gate A.** Latest order on that route: ~Dec 11–12 (arithmetic below). CJ also shows a galaxy projector with Bluetooth speaker tagged "US Warehouse" at $27.39 (search snippet, unclear which variant; would fail L-002 at $39 landed ~$28).
- Proof (all `estimated`, third-party): Amazon B0B4518KC2 ~9–10k units/mo at $36–44, 9.1–9.6K reviews (asinsight, Mar and Jun 2026); TikTok Shop sold counts for aurora projectors 34.6K ($32.37) and 22.6K ($12.99) (displayed on page); "northern lights projector" searches 2,900/mo in Sep 2025 vs 14,800 in Nov and 18,100 in Dec 2025 (RankHero, estimated, global, ~6x).
- Runner-up: 3-in-1 sunset/aurora/ocean lamp (Mubarek ~5k/mo on Amazon at $36.99). It lands at $13.60 on the China route and passes L-002 only at >= $40.80; Doba also lists ~10 in-stock US sunset lamps.
- Commodity floor: every item in this lane sells at $4–15 on Walmart/TikTok. A $39 price needs gift-ready packaging and a feature set. No Meta Ad Library check was possible (login wall).
- Compliance: reject remotes with CR2025/CR2032 coin cells; Bluetooth models need an FCC ID; position for 16+ only (CPSIA); at the brand stage run a lookalike/trademark check of any name or design against Northern Galaxy, OneFire, Airivo, Govee and Hatch.
- CJ US-warehouse outbound fee of $0.50 per parcel (knowledge/suppliers.md, from AliDrop/Spocket) must be added to any US-warehouse landed calculation. Duty is not added to US-stock goods (already imported), but the wholesaler's price carries it.
- Dismissed in this lane: candle-warmer lamps, flameless/rechargeable LED candles, throw/sherpa blankets, fuzzy slippers, tabletop trees, Christmas projector lights, moon lamps, curtain lights, USB fairy lights (appeal 3, add-on only).

## Brief
Owner wants a product that is an obvious Christmas present or self-purchase for the "Christmas-time feel", with no contrived micro-niche. Market US, budget under $500, custom site + Stripe + CJdropshipping (L-006). Must arrive before Dec 25. Price $25–60, landed <= 1/3 of price (L-002), light and unbreakable, demo-able in under 3 seconds (L-001). Landed-cost assumptions: duty 35% of unit cost (assumed), $1 clearance per parcel (assumed), China small-parcel shipping $5.50 up to 200 g, $6.50 at 250 g, ~$8 at 300–400 g (assumed; CJ calculator not reachable), payment fee 4.5% + $0.30. Landed = unit + shipping + 0.35 × unit + $1.
