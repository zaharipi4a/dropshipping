## Brief
- Owner: "focus on the idea of Christmas — a product that actually makes sense, that people buy as a Christmas present or for themselves for the Christmas-time feel." The nurse kit was rejected as contrived.
- Market: US. Owner outside the US. Custom website + Stripe + CJdropshipping (L-006). Budget under $500 total, organic-first.
- **Hard constraint: it must arrive before Dec 25.** Today is Oct 26. China shipping (7–15 business days + processing) leaves a cutoff around Nov 30. CJ **US-warehouse** stock (2–7 days) extends the selling window to ~Dec 15–18. US-warehouse availability is a major scoring factor.
- Must be giftable on sight (obvious appeal, no explaining), visual and demo-able on video, light and unbreakable to ship, priced $25–60 (or a bundle), landed ≤ 1/3 of price.
- Avoid: button/coin-cell batteries (Reese's Law), kids' toys (CPSIA), food and edibles, candles with flame/liquid fragrance shipping issues, fragile glass, trademarked characters/licensed designs, anything needing size variants.
